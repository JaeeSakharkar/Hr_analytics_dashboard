# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition and identify workforce trends across different demographic and organizational factors.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide an interactive view of employee attrition and help identify patterns based on:

- Employee demographics
- Age groups
- Education
- Salary ranges
- Job roles
- Years of experience
- Gender

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard provides an overview of important HR metrics, including:

- 👥 Total Employees: 1,470
- 📉 Employee Attrition: 237
- 📊 Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- 🎂 Average Age: 37 years
- 💰 Average Salary: 6.5K
- ⏳ Average Years: 7.0

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard visualizes:

- **Attrition by Education** – distribution of employee attrition across educational backgrounds.
- **Attrition by Age** – comparison of attrition across different age groups.
- **Attrition by Salary** – analysis of attrition across salary ranges.
- **Attrition by Job Role** – identification of job roles with higher employee attrition.
- **Attrition by Years** – analysis of attrition based on employee experience/tenure.
- **Attrition by Gender** – comparison of male and female employee attrition.
- **Job Role Breakdown** – detailed attrition count across different job roles.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

The dashboard transforms HR data into an interactive visual report that can help identify employee attrition patterns and provide insights into workforce trends.
